A topic I find interesting is the integration of AI into everyday society, and how the future will look like in the era of AI. Will we be in a situation like the movie WALL-E? Where all our needs will be met by technology, but we lose our sense of independence. And with AI restructuring how the workforce goes, will there be an universal basic income so that everyone can prosper, or will greed get in the way. This is something I'd like to explore more since we are in the transition period for the age before AI and going into an AI-forward age.
